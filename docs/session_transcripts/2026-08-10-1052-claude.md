## Four fixes

**1. Raster fallback for scans** (`_raster_content_rect`). When nothing is extractable, render the page at 50 dpi grey and bound the ink. Ink is judged relative to the page's own paper colour rather than a fixed cut-off — your Smoller scan photographs at ~210 grey, not white. Two robustness passes matter: solid scanner-bed bands get peeled off the edges, and isolated line-groups at either end get dropped. That second rule is what kills the 2px smear of 180-grey speckle down the weno scan's right edge, which was otherwise holding the crop at full page width. Result: a stable `x 0.164–0.855, y 0.157–0.874`, ~40 ms for 10 pages. The file now also *opens* already cropped.

**2. Figures were invisible to detection** — a separate, pre-existing bug I found while in there. `get_text("blocks")` is text-only; it omits images unless you ask, and never reports vector art. So a figure page cropped to its caption. On `jiang,shu--weno.pdf` p29 the old crop was `y 555–652` — a 97pt strip with the entire plot cut off. Now `get_bboxlog()` (0.2 ms/page) contributes figure bboxes, bucketed into an 8×8 grid because the clustering is O(n²) and one plot page has 6113 strokes.

**3. The cluster vote keeps every group ≥25% of the biggest**, not just the winner — once figures are in the running, a figure and the body that just miss the merge gap would otherwise discard whichever lost. Sampled `C` output on text papers is identical before and after; only per-page `Shift+C` changes.

**4. A stuck loading dot.** `_detect_generation` is bumped by manual crops too, so a zoom landing mid-detection sends the result down the superseded early-return — which happened *before* `_detect_inflight` was cleared, leaving the overlay's dot lit indefinitely. It's now a generation int (`_detect_pending_gen`) rather than a bool.

One perf note worth knowing: on a scan the expensive part is `get_text("blocks")` itself (~94 ms/page on the 664 MB Mcdonald scan), not my raster pass. Still, since `get_pixmap` holds the GIL for its whole duration, detection now runs text-only across all sampled pages first and rasterizes at most 4 of them as a fallback — rasterizing all 10 would be ~1.5 s of un-interruptible startup jank on your big scans.
